Search agent's summary (unverified): Mead's English version of the ascent rite in the great Paris magical papyrus. After an opening prayer the operator is told to draw breath from the sun's rays, inhaling three times as deeply as possible, and he will see himself lifted into mid-air; he will see the gods ascending and descending through the sun's disk and a pipe hanging from it. Hostile gods stare at him; he lays a finger on his lips and says 'Silence'. After hissing and puffing sounds the disk expands and a host of five-pointed stars comes out of it and fills the air. He is told at one point to close and then open his eyes, to see doors of fire opened, to gaze steadily and draw the divine breath into himself, and to bellow with belly-pressure 'to start thy senses going'. Youthful gods, lightning and a fiery god follow.
