Search agent's summary (unverified): Reichenbach's experimental record of persons who, in total darkness, saw flame-like and glowing light at the poles of magnets, on crystals, metals, plants and human hands. For practice the key points are his conditions. The light is extremely feeble; highly sensitive (often ill) subjects see it quickly, but the moderately sensitive must remain 'a long time, perhaps two hours, in absolute darkness' before their eyes are prepared, and during all that time not the smallest trace of other light may reach the eye. In Part II he reports healthy people who after one or two hours in his dark chamber were astonished to perceive many luminous phenomena they had never suspected, and describes how he picks likely subjects (people who dislike crowding, certain colours or sounds; a test pass over the palm). He records colours, sizes and forms of the lights and differences between observers.
