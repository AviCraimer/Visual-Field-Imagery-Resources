Search agent's summary (unverified): A working magician's handbook in which the commonest operation is divination by lamp or by a bowl of oil, usually through a boy medium but with repeated notes that the operator can do it alone. The pattern: a clean dark room or cell, a new lamp with clean wick and oil (or a vessel of oil), incense, the seer's eyes closed while an invocation is recited seven times 'down into his head', then eyes opened toward the lamp or oil. The first thing asked is whether he sees 'the light'; if he does, formulas bid the light grow and come in, and then the god or his shadow is seen about the lamp. If he does not see the light he is made to close his eyes again and a further formula ('O darkness, remove thyself') is recited, or he addresses the lamp himself. Several rites prescribe an eye ointment.
