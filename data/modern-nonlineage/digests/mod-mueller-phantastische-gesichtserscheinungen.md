Search agent's summary (unverified): The physiologist's first-person study of luminous images seen with closed eyes. He says he rarely fails to see manifold luminous images in the dark visual field before sleep, has known them since youth, and through repeated self-observation learned to promote and hold them. His procedure: with closed, completely rested eyes he looks into the darkness of the visual field, lets the eye muscles go slack, sinks into the stillness of the eye, and wards off all thought and judgment, only observing. At first the field is rich in light-spots, mists and shifting colours; soon bounded images of objects appear, faint and then clearer, really luminous and sometimes coloured, moving and transforming. The slightest eye movement, or reflection, dispels them. A later chapter asks whether such luminous phantasms can be imagined at will.
