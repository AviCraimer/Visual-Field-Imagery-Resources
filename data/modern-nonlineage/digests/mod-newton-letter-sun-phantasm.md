Search agent's summary (unverified): Newton tells Locke what happened when he looked briefly at the sun in a mirror with one eye and then watched the after-image in a dark corner, three times. As the 'phantasm of light and colours' was almost gone he 'intended his fancy' on it and found it returning as vivid as at first; when he stopped, it vanished. Thereafter he could bring it back in the dark by attending 'as when a man looks earnestly to see any thing which is difficult to be seen', more easily each time, until a bright round spot appeared on any bright object, and in the unexposed eye too. He shut himself in a darkened room for three days to recover; for months the image returned whenever he thought of it, even in bed at midnight.
