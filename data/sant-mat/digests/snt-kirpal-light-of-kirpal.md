Search agent's summary (unverified): Transcripts of informal question sessions at Sawan Ashram and Rajpur, 1969-71, in which Kirpal Singh goes round the room after sittings asking each Westerner what was seen and correcting them on the spot. Disciples report light of various colours, light that has grown dimmer, or nothing for days running; he replies with specific corrections (look into the middle of the light and it will open; do not visualise, it comes of itself; do not be anxious or make effort; the mind stands between). He ranks the colours (golden-yellow and white 'spiritual', red tied to a 'pushing attitude', blue as the common first light), gives the beginner's sequence of sky, stars, big star, moon, and uses the image of climbing a dark staircase to explain why many steps produce no light before the first glimpses.
