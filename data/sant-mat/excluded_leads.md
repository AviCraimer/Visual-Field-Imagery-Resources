- Rai Salig Ram, Radhasoami Mat Prakash (1896; archive.org radhasoamimatpr00unkngoog): public domain, but doctrinal; mentions the 'pupil of the eye' as seat of the spirit and inner impressions seen in practice, gives no procedure and no report of seen light. No practical content.
- Brahm Sankar Misra, Discourses on Radhasoami Faith (1909; archive.org discoursesonradh00misruoft): public domain; cosmology and apologetics; one quoted couplet on seeing the five tattva colours and 'flashes of the lightning of the moon' (art. 104-105) but practice explicitly withheld. No practical content.
- Kirpal Singh, Naam or Word (1960; archive.org naamorwordbykirpalsingh): anthology of scriptural testimonies to inner light and sound across religions; doctrine and proof-texts rather than procedure. Left out to avoid padding; a reader wanting Kirpal Singh's proof-texts for jyoti can find them there (publisher's upload, no rights reserved).
- Julian Johnson, With a Great Master in India (1934; archive.org dli.ernet.247203): letters home; devotional and descriptive, cosmology of regions; no instructions for or reports of seen light beyond generalities. No practical content.
- Jaimal Singh, Spiritual Letters (RSSB 1958/1967; archive.org SpiritualLettersFromBabaJaimalSingh): letters to Sawan Singh 1896-1903; stress bhajan and sound, almost nothing on seen light (23 mentions of 'light', all doctrinal or glossary). No practical content for this catalogue.
- Soami Ji (Shiv Dayal Singh), Sar Bachan Prose, Dayalbagh translation (1959; archive.org SarBachanRadhasoamiProse): cosmology and exhortation; the 'til' behind the eyes named as seat of the spirit, no procedure for or description of seen light in the prose. Metaphorical/doctrinal. (Sar Bachan Poetry, incl. the Hidayatnama, not examined: uploader-made PDFs of in-copyright Soami Bagh translations.)
- Shiv Brat Lal, Light on the Anand-Yog (Lahore 1931; archive.org LightOnAnandaYogaRadhaswamiDhamInstituteEdition): metaphysics of the Radhasoami regions; no practice instructions on seen light found by keyword skim.
- Ken Kelley, 'I See the Light', Penthouse, July 1974 (ex-premie.org/pages/penth_article.htm): fetched; only a one-line description of the four techniques ('pressing in the eyelids (Divine Light)') plus the account of the assault on Pat Halley; no instruction or report of seen light beyond that. No practical content.
- 'Is Meditation for Everyone?' (alt.support.ex-cult post, 22 Mar 97, on ex-premie.org/pages/ismeditation.htm): general meditation-harm piece; nothing specific to the light technique. Off topic.
