Best for induction: Sawan Singh (peep into the darkness; sparks, flashes), Kirpal Singh Spiritual Elixir and Teachings Bk 3 (gaze into the middle of darkness or light), Light of Kirpal (staircase image).
Best for development: Kirpal Singh letters and Sawan Singh (big star bursts, then sun, moon, radiant form); Mehi (drishti yoga to one-pointedness, then drop for sound).

Thin: DLM and Strasburg are reader extracts with no description of what is seen; Mehi says little about phenomena; digests only for in-copyright excerpts.
