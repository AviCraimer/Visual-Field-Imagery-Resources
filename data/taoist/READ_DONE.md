Verdicts: 5 keep, 0 keep_marginal, 0 drop (all five read).
Best for induction: dao-taiping-shengjun-mizhi (closed eyes dark at first; light arises of itself with long guarding; about 100 days); dao-jinhua-zongzhi-chinese (gaze and attention instructions, light enters by itself).
Best for development: dao-jinhua-zongzhi-chinese (nature-light vs consciousness-light), dao-dacheng-jieyao and dao-wu-shouyang-xianfo-hezong (three-appearance firing timeline), dao-huiming-jing-chinese (gathering light at emergence).
Disagreement: Taiping treats light as a direct fruit of simple guarding; Wu-Liu texts treat light as a late sign of completed firing and call earlier light delusion; Jinhua says not to care whether light appears.
Thin: digests for four sources held only prefaces, so passages came from one or two greps; Jinhua ch. 5 (errors) unread; Chinese only, no commentary.
