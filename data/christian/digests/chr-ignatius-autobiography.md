At Manresa (1522-23), during months of long daily prayer, severe fasting, vigils and penance, he repeatedly saw a beautiful indistinct shape 'of the form of a serpent with glittering eyes, and yet they were not eyes', which gave great consolation and left sadness when it went. He also reports a white object with rays, white rays descending at the elevation of the Host seen 'with the eyes of his soul', and the humanity of Christ as a white body without distinct members, seen 'perhaps twenty or even forty times'. After the illumination beside the river he saw the many-eyed thing again near a cross, noticed it had lost colour, judged it the devil's work, and thereafter drove it off with his staff. On the voyage to Jerusalem he saw something 'large, round, and golden'.
