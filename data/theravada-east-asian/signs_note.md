# Note: the three signs, and mind's eye versus visual field (existing records re-checked)

**Visuddhimagga (bud-visuddhimagga-nanamoli)**
- Learning sign (uggaha-nimitta): the earth disc is "adverted to now with eyes open, now with eyes shut" until "it comes into focus as he adverts with his eyes shut exactly as it does with his eyes open" (Ch. IV §§29-30, source lines 11110-11118). The sub-commentary glosses "comes into focus" as "becomes the resort of mind-door impulsion" (n. 9). Once it has arisen, stop looking at the disc, or the counterpart sign will not arise (n. 10, l. 11157).
- Counterpart sign (patibhaga-nimitta): "as if breaking out from the learning sign", purified, "like the moon's disk coming out from behind a cloud", yet "it has neither colour nor shape; for if it had, it would be cognizable by the eye"; "born only of perception ... a mere mode of appearance" (§31, l. 11135-11144).- Breath sign: star, gems, pearls, cotton, cobweb, sun or moon disc, varying "because of difference in perception"; cotton-type similes are learning sign, gem-type counterpart sign (Ch. VIII §§214-216 and n. 59, l. 20862-20898).

**Vimuttimagga (bud-vimuttimagga-ehara)**
- "Grasping sign" is taken by "even gazing", eyes neither wide nor shut, the sign seen "because of the mandala" like a face in a mirror (Ch. 8, l. 302-315).
- The "after-image" (counterpart) "can be seen with closed eyes as before ... only in thought"; it can be seen far, near, left, right, before, behind, within, without at will; "It appears together with mind" (l. 395-415). Located and willed, but "in thought".

**Pa-Auk, Knowing and Seeing (bud-pa-auk-knowing-and-seeing)**
- Breath: light may come before the nimitta and is distinct from it, "as the sun is different from sunlight". Dull white cotton-wool or dull ruby = uggaha; "bright like the morning star, brilliant and clear" = patibhaga (l. 2905-2927). A nimitta appearing "far away in front of you" should be ignored until it settles at the touch-point (l. 2992-2996).
- Earth kasina: gaze eyes open, then "close them, and visualize the earth kasina"; uggaha is when you see it "as clearly as if you were looking at it with your eyes open"; if you cannot visualise, use "the light of concentration" from a prior jhana (l. 5741-5760). So the sign is visualised but must reach eyes-open clarity.
- The light of concentration is explained as consciousness-born and temperature-born colour materiality (rupa-kalapas) (Q&A 4.10, l. 11915-11936).

**Fire Kasina (bud-fire-kasina-book-stein-ingram)**
- No uggaha/patibhaga vocabulary. Practice is staring at a flame, then with closed eyes seeing an after-image "red oblong dot presenting itself in the centre of the visual field" (l. 1657-1660); maintaining an "eyes-open after-image" between sits (l. 1836-1839). Glossary: nimitta is "mind-created" (l. 5751-5756). Practitioners speak of "mental colour" seen eyes open (l. 2976-2979) and a "mental visual field" (l. 4626).

**Summary:** classical sources put the counterpart sign at the mind-door; their learning-sign test is eyes-shut as clear as eyes-open. Only the fire-kasina practitioners say "visual field", hedged as "mental".
