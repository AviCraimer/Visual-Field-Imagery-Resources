Search agent's summary (unverified): The Pure Land visualisation sutra in its first English translation. The Buddha instructs Queen Vaidehi in a graded series of meditations. The first begins from physical sight: sit facing west, fix the mind and gaze at the setting sun 'when it is about to set and looks like a suspended drum', then let that image 'remain clear and fixed, whether thine eyes be shut or open'. The second proceeds the same way from clear water to ice to an imagined ground of shining lapis lazuli with jewels each sending out rays of many colours; the meditator is to make each element as clear as possible, hold it eyes shut or open at all times except sleep, and is told that this is to have 'dimly seen' the land, while one who obtains samadhi sees it 'clearly and distinctly'. Further meditations build jewel trees, lakes, the lotus seat and the radiant body of Amitayus.
