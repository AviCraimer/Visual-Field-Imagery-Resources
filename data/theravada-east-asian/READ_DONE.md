Verdicts: 8 keep, 1 keep_marginal (Mahasi), 0 drop; 9 of 9 priority-1 sources read, all from digests only.
Best for induction: bud-fire-kasina-book-stein-ingram (retinal after-image sequence, eyes-open carry-over), bud-pa-auk-knowing-and-seeing (breath nimitta to white kasina), bud-visuddhimagga-nanamoli and bud-vimuttimagga-ehara (kasina gazing to counterpart sign), bud-dhammakaya-luang-phor-sodh-manual (sphere visualisation, section 4.21 for those who see nothing), bud-contemplation-sutra-takakusu (setting-sun gaze retained eyes shut or open).
Best for development: bud-mn128-upakkilesa-sutta (eleven causes of vanishing light, limited vs limitless light), Pa-Auk (do not play with the nimitta, expansion in stages), Visuddhimagga Ch. XIII (renewing light through jhana).
Disagreement: Mahasi treats brilliant light as a corruption to be decided against, Pa-Auk and Visuddhimagga treat light as the working tool; Pa-Auk says the nimitta is produced by perception and may be altered by will in some passages yet forbids playing with it; Ajaan Lee calls signs inessential yet gives a method to enlarge and spread the white light.
Thin: Mahasi and MN128 give no induction; Ajaan Lee's Method 2 and seven steps, Pa-Auk Q&A on the light of concentration, Vimuttimagga breath section and Visuddhimagga Ch. IV and XX were not in the digests.
