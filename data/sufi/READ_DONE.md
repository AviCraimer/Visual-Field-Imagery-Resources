Best for induction: Tawakkul Beg (nights of heart concentration under a master's tawajjuh, bandaged-eye opening), Ghazali (Allah-Allah dhikr until the word's form drops, then wait), Ibn Arabi (retreat dhikr plus the eye-closing test).
Best for development: Razi (flashes, lamps, stars, moon, sun, colourless), Suhrawardi (eleven kinds of light with bodily effects), Rinn/Sanusi (seventy thousand lights in seven degrees).
Disagreement: Rinn and Razi grade lights by colour and stage, while Fleischer's author and Macdonald's note say the older masters taught no sequence and advise turning away; Ghazali and Tawakkul Beg make light a divine gift, not forceable.
Thin: Kubra and Razi digests missed the core text (low confidence, partly via search hints); few sources say whether eyes are open or closed, and none offers a way in for someone who sees nothing without a master.
