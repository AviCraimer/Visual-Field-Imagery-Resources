Search agent's summary (unverified): The Kubrawi handbook in Persian. The chapters saved cover the working method (dhikr of la ilaha illa'llah, how to sit and strike it, why it must be received from a shaykh, the conditions of the forty-day retreat in a small dark cell) and then what is seen. Chapter 17 says that as the heart is polished by dhikr lights appear: at first mostly as lightning-flashes and gleams, then as lamps, candles, torches and kindled fires, then as small and large stars, moon and sun, then as lights free of any locus. It assigns each form to a source (flashes mostly from dhikr and ablution; lamp-like light from the shaykh or the Qur'an; and so on), gives a sequence of colours tied to the stations of the soul ending in black light, and insists that colour and shape belong to the veils of imagination: the pure light has neither.
