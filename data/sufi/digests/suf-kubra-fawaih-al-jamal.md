Search agent's summary (unverified): The foundational Kubrawi treatise on what is seen in dhikr and retreat, written in numbered sections that mix instruction, interpretation and Kubra's own reports. It states the method as the 'way of Junayd' with eight standing conditions (ablution, fasting, silence, seclusion, the dhikr la ilaha illa'llah, heart bound to the shaykh, expelling thoughts, no objection to God), then describes what appears: darkness, then fire, then green; an open space with colours at the edge of vision, each colour read as a sign (green for the heart's life, clear red-fire for spiritual energy, yellow for weakness, blue for the nafs); flashes and lightning; circles of light at the eyes, between the eyebrows and by the nose; a black circle for the commanding soul; suns, moons and stars; the 'witness in heaven'. It distinguishes the fire of dhikr from the fire of Satan and gives tests.
