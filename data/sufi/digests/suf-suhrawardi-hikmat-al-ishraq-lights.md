Search agent's summary (unverified): The closing practical section of Suhrawardi's main work. He says that lights dawn on 'the brethren of detachment' and lists their kinds as experienced: a pleasant flashing light that comes to beginners and vanishes; a stronger, frightening flash that may come with a sound like thunder or a roar in the brain; a pleasant light arriving like hot water poured over the head; a long-lasting overpowering light with numbness in the brain; a very sweet light moved by love; a burning light; a brilliant light in a great flash, seen 'more clearly than the sun'; a light felt as hanging on the hair; lights with a grip on the head or in the brain; a light that clothes the whole body; lights with unbearable weight or with force that shakes the limbs. He ranks these as attainments of the intermediate. The Testament prescribes forty days of retreat without meat before the book is studied.
