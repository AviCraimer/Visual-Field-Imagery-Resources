Shadow-figure (304-313): stand alone with back to the sun, look attentively at the neck of one's shadow for as long as 108 calm repetitions of 'Om kram parabrahmane namah', then look up into the sky, where a figure appears; daily practice for six months and two years is specified, with omens read from its colour and missing limbs. In the essays Rama Prasad adds first-person testimony: after pranayama and trained attention he sees 'bright sheets' of green, white and red in the room, the sky, on a table or wall, and uses them to forecast rain; he says the faculty can be brought out by a sustained attempt to look into space, or at the moon, a star or a jewel.
