Search agent's summary (unverified): The standard hatha manual. Three places bear on seen light. (1) Trataka (2.31-32): gaze unblinking at a small mark until tears flow; presented as a cleansing act with no stated light result. (2) The Sambhavi-Unmani-Taraka group (4.35-41): attention on an inner target while the unblinking eyes look outward, then 'fix the gaze on the light' with brows slightly raised, and with half-closed eyes on the nose-tip, breath in ida and pingala stilled, 'he who can see the light which is the all, the seed' reaches the highest; said to be learnt only by a guru's favour. (3) Pancham Sinh's own 1915 introduction, a modern practitioner-style note, says that in contemplation one sees colours of the five elements, glittering stars and lightning flashes, first as agitated waves, then steady as 'one deep ocean of light'. The nada section (4.65 ff.) is about sound.
