Search agent's summary (unverified): Chapter 5 holds two induction methods. Pratikopasana (5.15-21): in a clear sunlit sky, at sunrise or by moonlight, fix the gaze steadily on the neck of one's own shadow, then look up into the sky, where a full grey figure is seen; this is an afterimage method, though the text treats the figure as divine and omen-bearing, and says that with constant practice one 'begins at last to see it in his heart'. Sense closure (5.22-26): ears stopped with thumbs, eyes with index fingers, nostrils with middle fingers, lips pressed with the rest, air confined; the yogi 'sees his soul in the shape of light', and seeing it unobstructed for a moment is said to free from sin; with gradual practice the inner sounds follow. Later verses of the chapter describe lights at the brow centre and elsewhere during chakra contemplation.
