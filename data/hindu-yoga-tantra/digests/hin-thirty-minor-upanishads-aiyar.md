Search agent's summary (unverified): The Mandalabrahmana Upanishad is the fullest classical statement of taraka yoga available in a public-domain English translation. It gives three lakshyas. Internal: close the ears with the forefinger tips, fix the mind on the booming sound heard, and a blue light is seen between the eyes and in the heart. External: colours seen in order at 4, 6, 8, 10 and 12 digits before the nose (blue, indigo-black, red wave, yellows); looking at space while moving the eyes, streaks of light at the eye-corners, after which vision can be steadied; a jyotis twelve digits above the head. Middle: variegated dawn colours in space, then five akashas. Brahmana II grades sight by eye aperture (closed, half open, fully open) and recommends the fully open gaze aimed at the nose-tip; first a deep darkness at the palate-root, then an endless sphere of light, with ordered signs from star to diamond, full moon, gems, midday sun and flame.
