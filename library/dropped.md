- **Shrichakrasambhara Tantra: A Buddhist Tantra (Tantrik Texts vol. VII)** (Kazi Dawa-Samdup (ed. and trans. of a Tibetan Chakrasamvara sadhana); ed. Arthur Avalon (John Woodroffe)) `vaj-chakrasambhara-1919`: imaginal_only. Generation-stage clarity is explicitly located in the mind: the mandala is to be concentrated on 'until it is vividly present to the mind's eye', meditated 'without any vagueness and dimness', and Avalon's introduction says the deities 'exist only in the mind'; useful as the converse case, not as a seen-light source.
- **Tantra of the Great Liberation (Mahanirvana Tantra)** (Anonymous tantra; introduction and commentary by Arthur Avalon) `hin-avalon-mahanirvana-tantra`: no_practical_content. Grep of the full text for yantra, gaze, gazing and unwinking found yantra only as a ritual object; trataka appears only as a one-line summary in the introduction with no seen-light claim.
- **Babylonian Talmud, Menachot 43b (R. Meir on tekhelet)** (Babylonian Talmud (baraita of R. Meir)) `jew-menachot-43b-tekhelet`: no_practical_content. R. Meir's tekhelet-sea-sky-Throne chain (segment [12]) is an associative or symbolic rationale for the colour, with no instruction to gaze for vision and no report of seen light; keep only as background to sapphire-light texts (cf. Evagrius, chr-evagrius-on-prayer).
- **Logos peri nepseos kai phylakes kardias (On Watchfulness and the Guarding of the Heart)** (Nikephoros the Monk (Nikephoros the Hesychast)) `chr-nikephoros-on-watchfulness`: no_practical_content. The method (breath led into the heart, Jesus Prayer, sitting) is described in full but no seen light is mentioned or promised; the only 'vision' word is a generic θεοπτία, so it fails the inclusion test, though it is the method that the Xanthopoulos and Gregory of Sinai light passages presuppose.
- **The Native Tribes of South-East Australia** (A. W. Howitt) `shm-howitt-native-tribes-se-australia`: off_topic. The initiation accounts (quartz crystals inserted, crystal water drunk, then seeing ghosts, journeys to Baiame) report visions of spirits, not light seen in the visual field; the only light references (a 'bright spark' bulk, a medicine-man's travelling light) are observed by others or are spontaneous, with no practice context.
- **A Description of the Western Islands of Scotland (incl. 'A Particular Account of the Second Sight')** (Martin Martin) `shm-martin-western-islands`: no_practical_content. The bards' dark-cell method (shut room, supine, stone on belly, plaid over the head, eyes covered, a whole day) aimed at verse composition and reports no seen light; the second-sight lights (spark of fire, corpse-candles) are spontaneous and not linked to any practice. Worth knowing as a sensory-restriction precedent only.
