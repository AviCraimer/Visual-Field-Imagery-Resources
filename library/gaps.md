# Gaps in the library

## Thin traditions and families

Tibetan material rests on two records: a secondary chapter on Togal and the bare Kalacakra root verses, whose method sits in an unread commentary. Dark retreat has no read source at all. Jewish practice has two records, and the Zohar passage may be a figure of speech. Shamanic material is two Inuit ethnographies, one confirmed only for a short passage. Taoism is weighted to one school (Wu-Liu), where light is a stage marker; the Shangqing sun and moon methods are unread. Hesychast and Eastern Christian sources are absent; the Christian set is Latin and English and almost wholly dissenting. The kasina family has five records, three of them classical. Sense closure has no confirmed first-person success that survived removal of the pressure.

## Families without an induction source

Breath and energy work (the alchemical manuals say light cannot be sought), and waiting on gift, by definition. Sufi seclusion has prescriptions but nothing addressed to the retreatant who sees nothing.

## Unconfirmed by digest reading

Kubra's eight conditions and closed-eyelid sign; Razi's forty-day dark cell; Reichenbach's two hours of dark adaptation; Mueller's eye-relaxation procedure; the Shiva Svarodaya time scales; the Siva Samhita finger-closure verses; Pa-Auk's Q&A on the light of concentration; Golden Flower chapter 5 on errors; Sagan's later chapters; the Visuddhimagga's full learning-sign instructions.

## Eight to read next

1. `vaj-geisshuesler-sky-gazing`: translates the primary Togal instructions that Baker only summarises.
2. `mod-galton-inquiries-human-faculty`: a self-described poor visualiser who trained himself to see things in the dark field; the exact case the brief asks about.
3. `bud-suttas-perception-of-light`: canonical perception-of-light instructions, the missing induction counterpart to MN 128.
4. `suf-ibn-ata-allah-miftah-al-falah`: dhikr method with posture, dark place and closed eyes; could supply the Sufi onset detail.
5. `snt-expremie-journey-ajw`: twenty-five years of practice with no light; the needed negative testimony.
6. `jew-abulafia-or-ha-sekhel`: an actual Jewish procedure with stated results.
7. `dao-zhengao-j9-10`: early Taoist eye and inner-viewing methods outside the alchemical frame.
8. `mod-bates-perfect-sight`: palming, where lights appear unwanted; bears on darkness gazing.

Also reread `suf-kubra-fawaih-al-jamal` in full; it is catalogued but at low confidence.
